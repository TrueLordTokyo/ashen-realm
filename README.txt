THE DRENCHWOOD

The forest is already in the file. You do not grow it. You do not press a button.
Open the file and the trees are there.

  1. Download Drenchwood.rbxlx again. Do not reuse the old file.
  2. Roblox Studio, File, Open from File.
  3. If you are looking at empty sky: in the Explorer window (right side),
     open Workspace, open Drenchwood, click the part named Lookout, press F.
     That frames the ridge. The trees are in front of you. The sea is to the right.
  4. Press Play to walk. You start on that ridge. Rain starts when you play.

No NPCs. No combat. Just the rain forest.
